A dialogue on nature-based solutions

Nature as Infrastructure Dialogue 2026 is a flagship knowledge-sharing initiative by Gamuda Land. It brings together experts, stakeholders and communities to exchange insights on nature-based solutions, sustainability and innovation.

The platform highlights a holistic approach to development, where biodiversity conservation, nature-based design and sustainable innovation are integral to long-term value creation. The event also showcases Gamuda's conservation efforts and on-the-ground initiatives across its developments, with expert-led discussions on integrating nature-based solutions into urban environments.

What to expect

Expert knowledge sharing

Gain insights from leading experts on nature-based solutions, biodiversity and sustainable development.

Panel discussions

Explore fresh perspectives and practical solutions through engaging conversations with industry experts.

Networking and social exchange

Connect with speakers and fellow participants, exchange ideas and build meaningful partnerships.

Project visits

Experience nature-based solutions in action through guided visits showcasing real-world applications.
